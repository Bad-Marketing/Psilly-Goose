Strategic Focus

Psilly Goose has built strong sales momentum since May, but growth has been heavily supported by advertising. The focus for the rest of 2026 should be to continue growing sales while improving overall profitability.

Refine the Offer
Opportunity

Offer refinement is the biggest growth opportunity for the rest of 2026.

The current offer is:

  • 4-pack at $32
  • $8 per can
  • No open coupon
  • 15% Subscribe & Save
  • No first-order Subscribe & Save coupon

The $32 price has proven it can convert, so we do not recommend immediately lowering the permanent price.

However, as the brand continues to grow, there is an opportunity to strengthen the offer against established competitors. BREZ sells at approximately $5 per can, while several competitors offer larger packs at a similar total purchase price. Strong Subscribe & Save acquisition offers are also used within the category.

Price/value is also a recurring theme within Psilly Goose's negative reviews.

The goal should be to maintain the premium positioning while creating stronger entry, repeat and value offers.

Introduce Larger Multipacks

Launch:

  • 8-pack at $49.99 / $6.25 per can
  • 12-pack at $59.99 / $5 per can

The larger packs give repeat customers a reason to increase commitment while improving value per can.

The 12-pack also brings Psilly Goose closer to BREZ's $5-per-can regular pricing without requiring the 4-pack to compete at the same price point.

The current sales performance gives us an opportunity to introduce a stronger pack structure while maintaining the premium pricing Psilly Goose has established.

Drive Subscribe & Save

Psilly Goose already has 112 active subscriptions with a 15% Subscribe & Save discount, despite not currently using an additional subscriber-acquisition coupon.

Test a 20% first-order Subscribe & Save coupon.

Rather than using an evergreen open coupon to discount one-time purchases, use promotional investment to encourage shoppers to subscribe.

BREZ currently uses a 30% first-order Subscribe & Save coupon in addition to its regular Subscribe & Save offer. Psilly Goose does not need to immediately match this. Start at 20% and increase only if subscriber growth and profitability support it.

The default non-event approach should be:

Protect Price → Incentivize Subscription → Build Repeat Revenue

Reserve broader open promotions for major Q4 acquisition events.

Improve Brand Demand Capture

Psilly Goose is generating branded search on Amazon, but there is an opportunity to capture more of that existing brand interest.

Search Query Performance shows room to improve both Impression Share and, particularly, Click Share across branded search terms. For shoppers specifically searching for Psilly Goose, the goal should be to dominate the search results and minimize traffic lost to competing products.

Strengthen Branded Search Capture

Focus on:

  • Maintaining strong organic positioning across branded terms.
  • Increasing branded Impression Share through appropriate advertising coverage.
  • Improving Click Share, with the offer as a key lever.
  • Building review volume to improve social proof against established competitors.
  • Monitoring competitor presence and offers across branded searches.
Expand Amazon Attribution

Use Amazon Attribution links consistently across eligible external traffic sources directing shoppers to Amazon, including:

  • Email
  • Website
  • Organic and paid social
  • Influencer / creator activity where applicable
  • Other off-Amazon campaigns

This will help Psilly Goose capture and measure more of the brand interest generated outside Amazon.

Improve Listing & Product Education
Launch Premium A+

Psilly Goose currently does not have Premium A+, creating a clear opportunity to strengthen the product detail page and improve product education.

Develop Premium A+ focused on:

  • What Psilly Goose is
  • Functional ingredients and formulation
  • Product differentiation
  • Intended experience
  • Alcohol-alternative positioning
  • Consumption occasions
  • Value behind the premium price
Align Product Expectations

A recurring negative-review theme is the difference between the expected and experienced effect.

Keep the Psilly Goose identity, but balance it with clearer product education and realistic expectations.

Review messaging across:

Listing → Advertising → Creator Content

Avoid over-relying on messaging that could create expectations of a strong or guaranteed effect. Instead, strengthen communication around the overall product experience, formulation, intended use and differentiation.

The goal is to reduce expectation-driven dissatisfaction without losing the brand personality.

Increase Review Velocity
Opportunity

Psilly Goose currently has approximately 70+ reviews at 3.8 stars.

The rating itself is relatively competitive within the category, with leading BREZ products reviewed sitting around 3.5–3.6 stars.

The bigger competitive gap is review volume, with established competitors carrying hundreds or 1,000+ reviews.

The priority should therefore be to increase review velocity and build stronger social proof, rather than focusing on materially changing the star rating.

Launch Product Insert

Develop an Amazon-compliant product insert encouraging customers to leave an honest review.

The insert should remain completely neutral:

  • No request for a positive review
  • No review incentive
  • No directing unhappy customers away from Amazon
  • No reward tied to leaving a review

A 53.7% CTR against 5.7% on the same intent is a placement problem. The Auto is winning Top of Search; the brand campaign is stacking up impressions further down the page.
